🚀 **New Research Project | Horizontal Federated Learning for VANET Security**

I’m working on an empirical study of privacy-preserving misbehavior detection in Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks (VANETs), using the VeReMi dataset to investigate the impact of data partitioning and federated learning architectures on detection performance.

🔬 **Current experimental implementation:**

* **Federated Learning Algorithms:** FedAvg and FedProx.
* **Architectures:** Flat Federated Learning and Hierarchical Federated Learning.
* **Data Partitioning:** IID, Non-IID, and Strong Quantity Skew.
* **Client Scalability:** Experiments with 5, 10, 15, and 20 clients.
* **Hierarchical Setup:** 5 Roadside Units (RSUs), with different client-to-RSU configurations.
* **Robustness Analysis:** Client dropout experiments with 20 clients.
* **Model:** Multilayer Perceptron (MLP) with three hidden layers (128, 64, and 32 neurons).

The experiments evaluate accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and training time per communication round to examine the effects of data heterogeneity, aggregation strategies, and hierarchical coordination on vehicular misbehavior detection.
